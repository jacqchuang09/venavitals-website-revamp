# Content Outline - Moment by Moment (First Draft)

The website laid out in the order a visitor experiences it, with the reasoning behind each choice tied back to the comparator sites we studied (doc 02). Wording is placeholder; we approve the shape and the logic first, then draft real copy.

**How to use the tables below:**
- The **Content Table** is the meeting view: every section, what our version shows, why we chose it (which reference drives it), and a slot to paste a reference screenshot.
- The **Reference vs Ours** table shows, per competitor, what their site does well and how our version matches or beats it.
- The **Value Proposition** tables are the message we are selling, by audience and against the alternatives.

This draft deliberately over-includes. It is easier to delete than to invent, so there is more here than the final site will use. `[OPT]` marks the first things to cut. `[VERIFY]` marks a claim to confirm before publishing.

# 1. VALUE PROPOSITION

**One line (placeholder):** *Arterial-line-grade blood pressure, continuous and noninvasive, from a sensor the size of a band-aid.*

### 1a. Value proposition by audience

| Audience (priority) | What they ask in the first 60 sec (doc 03) | Our value proposition to them | Proof we show, and where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hospital admins** (P1) | Regulatory status? Real evidence? Economic case? Who else uses it? | Arterial-line-grade BP without the line, with an honest evidence base and a low-risk pilot path | Evidence page, Partner economic case, backers row, 510(k) stated plainly (Hypervision playbook) |
| **Anesthesiologists** (P2) | Accurate vs A-line? Beat-to-beat waveform? Fits my workflow? | A continuous beat-to-beat arterial waveform, validated side-by-side vs A-line, placed out of the surgical field | Technology depth + per-claim citations (BioIntelliSense), spec table, the signal visual |
| **Researchers / Sleep** (P3, secondary) | Can I collaborate or run a study? | A research-grade platform with an open collaboration path | Evidence library + research-forward voice (Arcascope), framed as future direction |

### 1b. Value proposition vs the alternatives

The claim comparable to an arterial line is contested ground (BD Acumen IQ makes nearly the same claim), so we win by being specific about the difference. `[VERIFY every cell the team can defend.]`

| Dimension | Cuff (NIBP) | Arterial line | Finger-cuff (e.g. Acumen IQ) | **VeriTrack** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Invasiveness | Noninvasive | Invasive, procedure risk | Noninvasive | **Noninvasive** |
| Time to place | Seconds | 10-40 min, unpredictable | Quick | **Quick to apply** |
| Continuity | Snapshots every 3-5 min | Continuous beat-to-beat | Continuous | **Continuous beat-to-beat** |
| Placement | Upper arm | Radial artery | Finger (in surgical field, perfusion/squeeze) | **Foot, out of the surgical field** |
| What it shows | Intermittent numbers | Continuous waveform (gold standard) | Continuous + HPI prediction | **Continuous arterial waveform** |

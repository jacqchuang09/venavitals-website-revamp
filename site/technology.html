How It Works

Arterial-line insight. Band-aid form factor.

VeriTrack is built on a soft capacitive sensing stack developed at UC Irvine and grounded in peer-reviewed materials science. It captures beat-to-beat arterial pressure without puncturing skin, inflating a cuff, or occupying the arm.

Sensing Mechanism

How it works

The VeriTrack sensor sits over the dorsalis pedis artery on the foot. Its soft, stretchable material detects the subtle deflections of the artery beneath the skin with every heartbeat, converting that motion into a continuous blood pressure waveform: systolic, diastolic, and mean arterial pressure, beat to beat.

The sensing element is a wrinkled-gold (wAu) capacitive stack: a dielectric layer, silicone elastomer, air gap, and PDMS spacer. Each arterial pulse compresses the micropillar structure and changes the capacitance. An onboard algorithm converts those capacitance changes into continuous SBP, DBP, and MAP values with beat-to-beat resolution.

The Sensor

Soft enough to wear. Accurate enough to trust.

The VeriTrack sensor is thin, flexible, and skin-conforming. It moves with the patient through position changes and motion without losing signal. Biocompatible materials mean no skin irritation over the course of a case. Applied in under five minutes in pre-op, it stays in place and monitors continuously, without the placement time, complication risk, or nursing burden of an arterial line.

Clinical Workflow

Applied in pre-op. Out of the surgical field.

The sensor is placed on the foot before the case begins, in pre-op, before the patient enters the OR. It stays on through the entire case and is removed after emergence. No arm access, no sterile field disruption, no interval re-application.

1

Pre-op application

Apply the sensor to the dorsalis pedis artery on the foot in under 5 minutes.

2

Pair and confirm

Bluetooth connects to the iOS app. Signal quality confirmation on screen before incision.

3

Continuous monitoring

Beat-to-beat BP visible throughout the procedure, without the surgical field.

IP & Science

Patents and peer-reviewed science.

VeriTrack is built on a suite of patents protecting the wrinkled-metal sensing technology, grounded in peer-reviewed research published in Advanced Healthcare Materials (Kim et al., 2019). The foundational paper demonstrated beat-to-beat blood pressure sensing with a soft wearable sensor, the basis for the clinical system used in operating rooms today.

Kim et al., 2019

"Soft Wearable Pressure Sensors for Beat-to-Beat Blood Pressure Monitoring." Advanced Healthcare Materials

doi.org/10.1002/adhm.201900109

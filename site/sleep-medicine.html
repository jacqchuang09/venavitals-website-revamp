Sleep Medicine Research

Beyond the AHI: See the cardiovascular impact of every apnea.

VeriTrack is being studied as a research tool for continuous, noninvasive blood pressure monitoring during sleep.

The Opportunity

The nocturnal BP problem

Sleep-disordered breathing is a major cardiovascular risk factor, but the standard AHI captures breathing events, not their hemodynamic impact. Every apnea triggers a blood pressure surge. In patients with OSA, those surges compound across hundreds of events per night, year after year, driving hypertension, cardiac remodeling, and stroke risk. Measuring them continuously has not been practical until now.

What VeriTrack Captures

Beat-to-beat through the night

VeriTrack records beat-to-beat blood pressure throughout the night, capturing the magnitude and frequency of BP spikes triggered by apneic events. In preliminary research, OSA subjects showed 51 surges per hour above 30 mmHg, with spikes exceeding a delta of 80 mmHg, compared to 0 surges per hour in healthy subjects. A CPAP split-night study showed clear reduction in BP variability with therapeutic pressure.

The Case for Evaluation

The case for evaluation

Arterial lines carry procedural risk, require trained staff, and take time. Every line placed carries a 10 to 13% complication rate and a 0.6% infection risk. Placement time runs 5 to 20 minutes per case, time taken before the first incision. For high-volume ORs, the aggregate risk and cost of A-line placement is substantial. VeriTrack applies like a bandage and pairs with an existing iOS device. No new capital equipment, no line placement, no arterial access, and continuous beat-to-beat data from the first minute of the case.

Pilot Structure

A structured path to evaluation

1

Scope

Selected OR or ICU cases at your site, run alongside your existing monitoring setup. We work with you to define the patient population, endpoints, and review pathway upfront.

2

Prepare

We provide devices, setup support, onsite or remote training, and data flow confirmation. Your team learns the five-minute placement protocol before the first case.

3

Run

VeriTrack runs alongside your existing monitor. We provide onsite or remote support, weekly check-ins, and direct access to our clinical team throughout.

4

Review

At the end of the pilot, your site receives a full case-by-case data report: accuracy vs. concurrent monitoring, signal quality across patient demographics, workflow timing, and next-step recommendations.

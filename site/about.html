Our Story

Born at UCI. Built for the OR.

Vena Vitals began at UC Irvine, where Michelle Khine's lab developed a soft, stretchable material that captures highly sensitive cardiovascular data while moving like skin. Founded in 2019 [VERIFY], the team set out to close the gap between the cuff and the arterial line, and has since tested VeriTrack in operating rooms across the country. The mission: improve patient care through technologies that enable earlier detection and intervention.

# Master Plan

## What we are building

A clinical-grade marketing and diligence **website for Vena Vitals**, the company behind **VeriTrack** — a soft, band-aid-like sensor that measures continuous, beat-to-beat blood pressure without a cuff or an arterial line.

The current site is a single page that hides the company's two biggest assets: **600+ operating-room patients of validation data** and a **credible founding team**. The new site turns those into a credibility engine and a way to start sales conversations.

## Why we are building it

- Make Vena Vitals look real, credible, and ready to evaluate.
- Surface the evidence and the team that the current site hides.
- Give the right people an easy way to **request a demo or start a pilot**.
- Be honest about stage: the device is **submitted for FDA 510(k), not yet cleared**.

## Who it is for (priority order)

| Priority | Audience | What they want |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hospital administrators / buyers** | What it does, regulatory status, real evidence, the economic case, a way to talk to a human. |
| 2 | **Anesthesiologists (clinical champions)** | How it works, accuracy vs the arterial line, waveform quality, how it fits their workflow. |
| 3 | **Researchers / Sleep (secondary)** | Evidence and a way to collaborate. Framed as future direction. |

Consumers are **not** a target. No consumer-wellness look or language. (Full detail: `../03-audiences.md`.)

## The product, in one paragraph

VeriTrack is a soft, flexible capacitive sensor using patented wrinkled-metal technology. Placed on the skin over a palpable artery (currently the dorsalis pedis, on the foot), it streams continuous arterial blood pressure to a tablet over Bluetooth. Tested on 600+ OR patients against side-by-side arterial lines. Backed by Y Combinator, MedTech Innovator, EvoNexus, and others.

## The one thing the site must do

**Primary goal:** get qualified visitors to **Request a Demo**.
**Secondary:** download the one-pager / sign up for updates; spend time on the Clinical Evidence page.

## What this site is NOT

- Not a consumer or patient product site.
- Not a place that claims FDA clearance, availability, or clinical outcomes we cannot back up.
- Not a redesign that adds features nobody asked for. Match the plan; do not improvise.

## Success looks like

- Demo requests per month (primary).
- One-pager downloads / update sign-ups (secondary).
- Time on the Clinical Evidence page (a proxy for clinician interest).

## The lines we hold

This is the one short list of non-negotiables. They are **legal and clinical, not stylistic** — keep them exact. Everywhere outside this list, you have full freedom to innovate.

1. Never say or imply FDA-cleared, approved, or commercially available. Standard line on every product page: *VeriTrack has been submitted for FDA 510(k) review and is not yet available for commercial sale.*
2. "Comparable to the arterial line" only next to the validation citation.
3. No outcome claims (mortality, AKI, etc.) — we do not have that data.
4. Sleep and home uses are **future directions**, not products.
5. When a fact is uncertain, confirm it with a human before it ships rather than guessing.

# User Journeys

What each visitor does, in order, and which page/section serves each step. Build so these paths are obvious and short. Every journey ends at **Request a Demo**.

## Journey 1 — Hospital administrator (priority 1)

A buyer doing field research before or during a purchase evaluation. Allergic to hype; wants proof and a path to act.

| Step | What they do | What they need | Where on the site |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Land and size it up in ~60 seconds | What it is, why it matters, that it is credible | Home: hero + trust bar |
| 2 | Understand the problem and the fix | The cuff/A-line gap, and how VeriTrack closes it | Home: problem + solution + see-it-in-use |
| 3 | Check the proof | Real validation data, honest regulatory status | Home: evidence strip → **Clinical Evidence** page |
| 4 | Build the business case | Economic case, what a pilot looks like, who else uses it | **Partner With Us** |
| 5 | Confirm the company is real | Founders, advisors, backers | **About** |
| 6 | Act | An easy way to talk to a human | **Request a Demo** (persistent button + Partner form) |

**Design implications:** the homepage hero must work for this person first. Keep regulatory status visible and honest. Give them something to circulate internally (one-pager PDF).

## Journey 2 — Anesthesiologist (priority 2)

The clinical champion who pushes admin to evaluate. Skeptical, data-literate, time-poor.

| Step | What they do | What they need | Where on the site |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Land | Quick sense of what it is | Home: hero |
| 2 | Go straight to how it works | Sensor principle, placement, waveform quality, the technical depth | **Technology** (simple on top, detailed below) |
| 3 | Scrutinize the evidence | Accuracy vs the arterial line, methodology, citations, publication list | **Clinical Evidence** |
| 4 | Check workflow fit | Setup time, out-of-field placement, monitor integration | Technology: workflow + Solutions: Operating Room |
| 5 | Trust the people | Named clinical advisors and investigators | Clinical Evidence: advisors / About |
| 6 | Act | Participate or evaluate | **Request a Demo** |

**Design implications:** do not dumb down Technology or Evidence. Per-claim citations are the strongest trust device for this audience.

## Journey 3 — Researcher / Sleep (secondary)

Brief. Served by the Clinical Evidence page and a collaboration path; Sleep is framed as a future direction, not a product.

| Step | What they do | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Land, self-select | Home: audience cards |
| 2 | Read the science and publications | Clinical Evidence |
| 3 | Reach out to collaborate | Contact / Request a Demo |

## What this means for every page

- The **Request a Demo** button is persistent in the header on every page.
- Secondary CTA is **See the Evidence**.
- Every page should make the next step obvious — never a dead end.

# Learnings (self-improvement log)

_What worked, what slowed us down, and what to change. This is where the project teaches itself. Newest at the top._

_Rule of thumb: if the same friction shows up twice, it belongs here — and the fix belongs in the doc that was wrong._

Entry template:

```
## <YYYY-MM-DD> — <short title>
- **What happened:** <the friction, the surprise, the mistake>
- **Why:** <root cause, not just the symptom>
- **Change to make:** <the concrete fix>
- **Where:** <which doc/rule to update — and whether it has been applied yet>
```

## Retro checklist (run every few sessions, or when asked)

- What slowed us down more than once?
- Which doc was misleading or out of date?
- What rule did we keep bending? (Either follow it or change it.)
- What estimate was off, and why?
- What is now safe to delete from the docs?

After the retro: apply the fixes to `RULES.md` and the plans, and note what changed below.

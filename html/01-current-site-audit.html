Company facts NOT on the site (found via search — verify before publishing)

  • Founded 2019; founders: Ray Liu (CEO), Prof. Michelle Khine (UC Irvine Biomedical Engineering — technology inventor), Josh Kim (developed tech in his PhD, lead author on the seminal papers)
  • Tested on 600+ patients in operating rooms across the country; side-by-side comparison against arterial lines showed the device "matched rapid blood pressure changes almost perfectly"
  • FDA 510(k) submitted; clearance anticipated 2026
  • Backers/accelerators: Y Combinator, MedTech Innovator, EvoNexus, federal grants, UCI Beall PoP grant; investors incl. Samsung NEXT, Battle Born Venture, Morado Ventures (per Crunchbase, ~$2M+ raised — confirm internally)
  • ~14 employees, 9 UCI alumni incl. 5 PhDs; labs at the Cove at UCI and University Lab Partners
  • ~30 anesthesiologist customer-discovery interviews
  • Quotable founder lines (Ray Liu, UCI Beall article):
  • "Our blood pressures are constantly changing, but we only get snapshots in time using uncomfortable cuff compressions, or we need risky invasive procedures to track real-time changes."
  • "They told us this could really change how they manage patients. Especially since arterial lines are invasive, introduce patient risk, and have unpredictable procedure times."
  • "We're the first and only technology that can quantify the magnitude of blood pressure spikes immediately after apneic events."
  • "There's a lot of noise in the wearable space. But we've always been about clinical impact."
  • Problem framing: A-line placement takes 10–40 min and is unpredictable, delaying surgery starts; cuffs give snapshots only

Gaps vs. what a hospital buyer / anesthesiologist needs

  • 1. No clinical evidence page — the single most important missing asset. 600+ OR patients and A-line comparison data exist but aren't shown. No publications list despite peer-reviewed papers existing.
  • 2. No real pages — a one-pager reads as "early stage / not ready to buy from." Multi-page architecture itself is a credibility signal.
  • 3. No demo/pilot CTA — only a mailto. No form, no "Request a Demo," no pilot-program path, so no lead capture at all.
  • 4. No team/advisors page — the founding story (UCI professor + PhD inventor + experienced medtech CEO) is a major trust asset, currently invisible.
  • 5. No news/press page — YC, MedTech Innovator, funding, publications all unannounced on-site.
  • 6. No product page identity — "VeriTrack" is mentioned in passing; it needs its own named product presentation with images and specs.
  • 7. No downloadable assets — admins circulate PDFs internally; there's nothing to circulate.
  • 8. Regulatory status buried — pre-clearance status should be stated clearly and confidently (it's normal for the stage), not as an aside.
  • 9. No imagery of the device in clinical use — comparators all show the product on a patient in a real setting.

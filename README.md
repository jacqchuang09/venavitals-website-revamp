Created June 11, 2026. Research basis: live fetch of venavitals.com, web search for company facts, and teardowns of the team's comparator list - Proprio, Arcascope, Caretaker Medical, BD Acumen IQ Cuff, BioIntelliSense, Accuryn, Hypervision Surgical - plus design references (Eko, Butterfly, Biobeat, Retia). **Build platform: Wix Studio** (team constraint; all requirements map to native Wix features - see file 04).

## Files (read in order)

1. **01 - Current Site Audit** - what's on venavitals.com today, reusable copy, company facts found via research, and the 9 gaps to close.

7. **07 - Content Outline (Moment by Moment)** - the site in the exact order a visitor experiences it. Three views: a **Content Table** (the meeting view - every page/section, what goes there, who supplies the asset), a **Page Map** (skim), and a **Detailed Outline** (each moment's job, on-screen display, placeholder copy, asset + source). 8. **08 - Questions & Approvals (One Pager)** - everything needing a decision or sign-off before build: regulatory wording, which evidence is public, assets to request, platform. Grouped by owner to run fast in the meeting.
9. **09 - Project Outline (2 Weeks) + Next Steps** - day-by-day plan to a first-draft site, critical path, and immediate next steps.

## The one-paragraph strategy

The current one-page site hides Vena Vitals' two biggest assets: **600+ OR patients of validation data** and a **credible founding team**. The revamp turns the site into a diligence tool for hospital administrators (economic case, pilot path, demo form) and a credibility engine for anesthesiologists (deep technology page, filterable clinical-evidence library) - following the structural playbook of Caretaker Medical and Eko Health while being more transparent about evidence and regulatory status than competitors like Retia. Primary conversion: **Request a Demo**. Honest pre-clearance positioning throughout: "FDA 510(k) submitted."
